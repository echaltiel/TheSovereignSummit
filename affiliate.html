How it works.

01

Share the affiliate code assigned by the Summit team. Buyers enter your code at checkout for 10% off their ticket purchase, and the sale is attributed to you.

Commission Payments / Required Documentation

Payouts & deadlines.

Total commissions are paid 60 days after the conference ends. For affiliates earning $600 or more in total commissions, payment requires timely submission of a completed Form W-9.

W-9 requirement

Under this program, a completed Form W-9 must be submitted to receive total commissions of $600 or more. At the 10% commission rate, this threshold is reached at $6,000 in net ticket sales.

Missed deadline: commissions are forfeited

If your total commissions are $600 or more and you do not submit the required completed Form W-9 by March 9, 2027, all of your commissions are forfeited.

Flight receipts due March 9, 2027

Flight receipts must be received within 30 days after the conference ends, by March 9, 2027. Flight reimbursement is up to $600.

The Fine Print

Program terms.

Eligible tickets
Summit Pass and VIP Summit Pass purchases made with your code. Kids passes, hotel rooms, side-event tickets and sponsorships are excluded.
Net sales
Ticket revenue after the buyer discount, fees, refunds and chargebacks. Any sales tax collected is excluded.
Enrollment
By approval. Request a code through the form below. The Summit team assigns one code per affiliate.
Your own purchases
Tickets you buy yourself do not count toward commission or reward tiers.
Other discounts
Your affiliate code cannot be combined with any other discount or promotion.
Sales deadline
Sales count through February 3, 2027 at 11:59 pm ET.
Payment method
ACH, check or Bitcoin, at your choice. There is no minimum payout.
Tax forms
Request a secure upload link from the Summit team for your Form W-9. Do not send tax forms by plain email. Affiliates outside the U.S. submit Form W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E in place of the W-9, by the same March 9, 2027 deadline.
Refunds and chargebacks
Commission is calculated on final net sales. A pass or travel reward already used is not taken back. A reward not yet used can be withdrawn if refunds or chargebacks bring you below its tier.
Passes
Issued within 7 days of reaching a tier. Passes may be transferred to a named guest. They have no cash value and may not be resold.
Flight reimbursement
Once per affiliate, for your own travel to the Summit, up to $600. Send receipts to info@thesovereignsummit.com by March 9, 2027. Reimbursed with commissions on April 8, 2027.
Hotel
Once per affiliate. Three nights between February 4 and 7, 2027 in a standard room at Fontainebleau Miami Beach, booked by the Summit team. Room rate, taxes and resort fee are covered. Incidentals are not.
Promotion rules
Disclose that you earn commission. Do not post your code on coupon or deal sites, and do not run paid ads that bid on the Summit’s name.
Program changes
The Summit may change or end the program with written notice by email. Commission and rewards already earned are honored.
