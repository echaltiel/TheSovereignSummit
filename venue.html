Coastal Convention Center

An Iconic Miami Landmark

Twenty oceanfront acres on Collins Avenue. Designed by Morris Lapidus in 1954, named to the National Register of Historic Places, and woven into American glamour ever since — from Goldfinger to Sinatra at La Ronde to a billion-dollar restoration that preserved every curve. Seventy years in, it is still the spot everyone in Miami wants to be seen in.

Fontainebleau was chosen because the best events do not end when the sessions do. They keep unfolding in the lobby, over dinner, by the pool, at the bar, and in the late-night conversations no agenda can manufacture. The Sovereign Summit is built around that kind of energy: an integrated resort experience where many attendees are staying on property, major moments happen on site, and the community stays together instead of scattering across the city. That is what makes Fontainebleau different. It turns the venue into the social engine of the Summit.

Getting Here
Arrivals Drive @ Fontainebleau ↗

Follow signs for the Coastal Convention Center, in the same direction as the Trésor Tower. Near the Trésor Tower, take the elevator labeled "Coastal Convention Center." Select the Skybridge Level. Walk across the skybridge, which will bring you directly to the third floor of the Coastal Convention Center.

Departures Drive @ Fontainebleau ↗

As you pull into the area, look for the Coastal Convention Center sign on the building that's on the right-hand side. Walk through the doors toward the Trésor Tower area. Take the elevator labeled "Coastal Convention Center." Select the Skybridge Level. Walk across the skybridge, which will bring you directly to the third floor of the Coastal Convention Center.

Collins Avenue Walk-Up ↗

On Collins Avenue, directly across from Soho Beach House, look for the pedestrian entrance to the Coastal Convention Center. Walk through the doors and take the elevator or stairs up. Note: you'll be denied entry without your credentials. If you don't have them yet, use another entrance.
