Nº 06 — Venue

Fontainebleau. The Summit Campus.

Coastal Convention Center

An Iconic Miami Landmark

Twenty oceanfront acres on Collins Avenue. Designed by Morris Lapidus in 1954, named to the National Register of Historic Places, and woven into American glamour ever since — from Goldfinger to Sinatra at La Ronde to a billion-dollar restoration that preserved every curve. Seventy years in, it is still the spot everyone in Miami wants to be seen in.

Fontainebleau was chosen because the best events do not end when the sessions do. They keep unfolding in the lobby, over dinner, by the pool, at the bar, and in the late-night conversations no agenda can manufacture. The Sovereign Summit is built around that kind of energy: an integrated resort experience where many attendees are staying on property, major moments happen on site, and the community stays together instead of scattering across the city. That is what makes Fontainebleau different. It turns the venue into the social engine of the Summit.

Nº 08 — FAQ

Everything you need to know.

01What is The Sovereign Summit?

A two-day, single-stage conference at the Fontainebleau in Miami Beach, Florida — Friday, February 5 and Saturday, February 6, 2027. It gathers builders, founders, allocators, researchers, and serious thinkers for world-class talks, panels, and unscripted conversations across four themes: Energy, AI, Freedom, and Bitcoin. Programming runs 10 AM – 5 PM each day, followed by curated evening side events.

02Who is this event for?

Anyone seriously interested in where energy, AI, freedom, and Bitcoin are heading. The speaker lineup and agenda are built for builders, founders, operators, allocators, researchers, and engaged learners — people who want to be in the room where these conversations are actually happening, not just watching them from a distance.

03Is this a crypto conference?

No. The Summit is explicitly described as a Bitcoin experience — and monetary policy is just one of four themes. Energy, AI, and freedom each get their own dedicated program block and attract attendees who may have no Bitcoin exposure at all. The event is about the convergence of these ideas, not token markets.

04Why these four topics together?

The agenda treats them as one interlocking system, not four separate topics. The opening block — "The Inflection Point" — frames why energy, money, intelligence, and freedom are all shifting simultaneously. "The Convergence" then explores how they reinforce each other. The Summit is built around the idea that the most important conversations happen at those intersections, and that understanding any one of these forces in isolation misses the larger picture.

05How is this different from other conferences?

Scale and integration. Two days, one stage, a single hotel — with many attendees staying on property. Fontainebleau was specifically chosen because the best events don't end when the sessions do: they keep unfolding in the lobby, over dinner, by the pool, and at the bar. The venue is designed to be the social engine of the week, not just a backdrop for it.

06This is the inaugural year. Why should I trust it?

A few concrete signals: a confirmed venue at one of Miami Beach's most iconic properties, a publicly listed speaker announcement cadence, transparent tiered pricing with no hidden fees, and a clearly stated full-refund policy through December 1, 2026 with free ticket transfers through January 28, 2027. The event is sized for quality over scale — roughly 400 GA seats — which is a deliberate bet on an exceptional first edition over a big one.

07What are the dates and hours?

The Summit runs Friday, February 5 and Saturday, February 6, 2027, from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM each day. Side events begin the evening before (Thursday, February 4) and wrap up Saturday night.

08What's included with every ticket?

Every summit ticket includes the full two-day program — all main-stage sessions both Friday and Saturday — plus the Thursday evening Bitcoin Fundamentals session. Breakfast, lunch, coffee, and drinks are included throughout each summit day. GA phases (Early Bird, Standard, Final Release) differ only in price and timing; the seat and program are identical.

09What does VIP admission include?

VIP admission ($2,495 · limited to 200 spots) includes reserved front-row seating for both summit days, speaker green-room access, VIP-only access to the Welcome Reception on Thursday and the Oceanfront Soiree on Friday, and access to the Saturday night Boat After Party.

10When is the full agenda published?

The full agenda will be published closer to the event. Speakers are announced on a rolling basis. Subscribe to the email list at the bottom of the site — or follow @sovereignmiami on X — to get updates as they're released.

11What side events are there?

Thu Feb 4 · 5–6:30 PM — Bitcoin Fundamentals — included with every ticket. Three 30-minute sessions on bitcoin basics, mining, and its importance for human freedom. Coastal Convention Center inside Fontainebleau. First come, first served.

Thu Feb 4 · 7–10 PM — Welcome Reception — VIP ticket only. Private evening at Arkadia Day Club inside Fontainebleau. Tray-passed food and open bar.

Fri Feb 5 · 7–10 PM — Oceanfront Soiree — VIP ticket only. Drinks, dinner, and ocean views at La Côte inside Fontainebleau. Food buffet and open bar.

Sat Feb 6 · 7–10 PM — Boat After Party — included with VIP; available as an add-on for GA. An evening on Biscayne Bay aboard Biscayne Bay Yacht Charters with food, open bar, and Miami skyline views. Shuttles run from the Fontainebleau lobby to Bayside Marina. Limited to 400 total guests.

More side events are being added — check the Side Events section for the latest, and subscribe to the newsletter to be notified as new ones are announced.

12Where do side events take place?

Three of the four are on the Fontainebleau property: Bitcoin Fundamentals in the Coastal Convention Center, the Welcome Reception at Arkadia Day Club, and the Oceanfront Soiree at La Côte. The Boat After Party departs from Bayside Marina — about 25 minutes away — with shuttles running from the Fontainebleau lobby.

13Can I pay with bitcoin?

Of course. Checkout supports on-chain bitcoin, Lightning, and card. Lightning is fastest and our preferred method. The ticket is identical either way.

14What's the refund and transfer policy?

Summit tickets are fully refundable through December 1, 2026, and transferable through January 28, 2027 — if you can't make it, send someone else. All refunds are issued as cash value, including tickets purchased with bitcoin.

Side event tickets are refundable up to ten days before each event.

Refund or transfer questions → info@thesovereignsummit.com.

15Where should I stay?

A preferred-rate room block is held at the Fontainebleau itself — the booking link is in the Venue section.

If you'd rather stay elsewhere, our travel partner Victoire Voyages can secure preferred rates at most Miami Beach and downtown properties.

16What's the dress code?

Smart casual, Miami Beach edition. No suit required, but save the beach shorts and flip-flops for the pool. Dress like you're going to a beautiful hotel to meet serious people — because you are.

17Accessibility, dietary, and other accommodations?

Fontainebleau is ADA-accessible — one of the reasons we chose it. We'll do our best with dietary needs, accessibility requests, nursing rooms, prayer space, and quiet rooms — anything that helps you have a better experience.

Email info@thesovereignsummit.com. The earlier we know, the more we can do.

18Are there sponsorship opportunities?

Yes — selectively. We work with a small set of sponsors that fit the room and add real value to attendees. Tiers include Platinum, Gold, Silver, and Activation-level spots (badge, lanyard, coffee & water, bag, exhibition table, side events, and more).

See the Sponsors section or email info@thesovereignsummit.com for the deck.

19Still have questions?

Email info@thesovereignsummit.com or DM @sovereignmiami on X. A real person answers, usually within a day.
