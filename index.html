Erick Brimen

Founder & CEO
Próspera
Founder & CEOPróspera

Erick Brimen is the founder and CEO of Próspera, the world’s most advanced governance-as-a-service jurisdiction, operating on the island of Roatán, Honduras. Born in Venezuela, he’s seen firsthand what happens when opportunity exists, and what happens when it doesn’t. That contrast has driven everything since.

His career before Próspera spans private banking at Brown Brothers Harriman, M&A advisory at AG Edwards and Ernst & Young’s London practice, real estate development across Latin America, and co-founding ComparaMejor.com, Colombia’s leading online insurance brokerage. In 2013, he began channeling that experience toward a single thesis: that better governance, properly structured, is the highest-leverage intervention in human development.

Próspera is that thesis operationalized. A functioning special economic zone with its own legal code, regulatory framework, and growing community of residents and businesses, it runs under a multi-decade stabilization agreement backed by international treaty, specifically designed to attract capital to a region that scares it away. The project has survived adversarial governments and constitutional challenges, which only validated the core premise: that private governance, when built right, is more durable than political cycles.

His long-term ambition extends well beyond one island. Erick is working to replicate the model across a network of zones, creating a platform for U.S. and global capital to invest in Latin America at scale, while directly expanding access to opportunity for the communities where Próspera operates. A member of the Society for International Development in Washington DC, his goal has always been the same: make human flourishing systematic, not accidental.

@erickbrimenprospera.co
