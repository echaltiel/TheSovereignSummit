Sponsor FAQ

The important details, up front.

01Are Title and Gold programming opportunities guaranteed?

Yes. The programming opportunity is guaranteed. The final topic, format, participating speakers, claims, and content remain subject to approval by The Sovereign Summit.

02Do sponsorships include category exclusivity?

No. The Sovereign Summit is built around individual choice. We want attendees to encounter competing ideas, products, and solutions and decide what works best for them. We approve sponsors for fit, but do not prevent other companies in the same category from participating.

03What is included in the booth package?

The standard footprint, branded backwall, package furniture, standard power, and hardwired internet. Custom construction and additional services are scoped separately.

04How is booth placement determined?

Sponsors select locations within their sponsorship level in the order completed agreements and deposits are received. All locations remain subject to the final event and expo plan.

05Can a package be customized?

Yes. Media, hospitality, programming, and activation opportunities can be combined with any confirmed sponsorship level, subject to availability and final approval.
